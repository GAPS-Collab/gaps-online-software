\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
The \doxylink{structgondola_1_1MasterTriggerEvent}{Master\+Trigger\+Event} represesnts the information provided by the MTB for this one specific event. Most notably, it includes a board mask, which is the DSI/J connections which triggered, and a hit mask. The hit mask gives hit channels per DSI/J, which correspond to hit channels on a LTB.
