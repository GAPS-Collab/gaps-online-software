Searchlight - the reconstruction of the primary track

The cleaned linefit is seeded through the earliest outer and the earliest inner TOF hit. When the primary’s paddle is not read out, one of these seed hits belongs to a secondary, and the fit goes astray. Searchlight flags such seed hits by their timing and energy, and replaces them by other points: the vertex (the stopping point of the primary, found from the secondaries like a searchlight sweeping back to their origin), high-energy tracker hits and triggered paddles without a hit. Each candidate line is a cleaned fit, and a logistic regression over a few features of the candidates picks one.

All lengths are in mm, times in ns, energies in MeV.
