Secondary tracks and timing for the vertex of searchlight

A RANSAC track finder with the anisotropic errors of the tracker strips: a strip hit measures one coordinate well (across the strip) and the other only to within the length of the strip. Track candidates start from two hits in different layers, take all free hits within the tolerances, are fitted with per-axis errors, and the best ones are taken greedily.

The timing score: every secondary TOF hit gives an estimate of the stopping time of the primary, t - |hit - vertex|/c, and a good vertex has several of them agreeing within the bounds the primary allows.
