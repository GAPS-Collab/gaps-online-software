\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{structRBMissingHit}{RBMissing\+Hit} represents missing data from a readoutboard. This can occur when the MTB has a hit registered, but for some reason we do not get the corresponding data for the RB. \doxylink{structRBMissingHit}{RBMissing\+Hit} might help with debugging.
